What Makes Our Committees Unique?

TCET MUN offers carefully curated committees that simulate real-world international organisations. Every committee provides delegates with opportunities to strengthen diplomacy, leadership, negotiation and public speaking while debating some of the world's most pressing issues.

Global Perspectives
